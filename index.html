Squad Potential

For each active tactic slot, every squad player scored 0-100 on how good they could become in that exact role — blending their scouted Potential rating, current attribute fit, age headroom, and any growth-related hidden attributes (Determination, Professionalism, Adaptability, Consistency, Ambition, Important Matches, Versatility, Injury Proneness) your export includes. Missing columns are skipped, not guessed at.
